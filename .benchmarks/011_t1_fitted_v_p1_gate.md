# Bench 011 — Issue 013 T1: the fitted-V P1 gate on gemma-2-2b (MEASURED NEGATIVE for P1 promotion; a KVarN V-posture finding)

**Status:** COMPLETE — the model-bound P1 G1 gate (riir-infer Issue 013 T1, katgpt-rs Issue 883 P1 / Bench 895) is a **measured NEGATIVE on gemma-2-2b**: token-mean-removed V quantization (`MeanRemovedValueCache` over KVarN) does not beat plain V quant at matched bits on PPL — PASS at 2 bits by a within-noise margin (−0.022% Δppl, 7/12 chunk win share), FAIL at 3 bits (+1.717%) and 4 bits (+0.489%). The pre-registered `1 − ρ_l(V)` MSE-drop prediction OVERSHOOTS what mean removal delivers on real rows (Gate 1 FAIL at 3/4 bits: within ±0.20 on 15/26 layers; the ratio sits ABOVE the prediction nearly everywhere). **P1 is not promotable on this fixture; the recorded negative is the gate's legitimate answer** (the issue's own promotion statement). The P1/P2/T2+ work continues unblocked by this (T2's K=V+ λ ladder is a different product — its question is quality(K=V+) vs quality(K=V), not quantization).

**Second result (upstream-relevant, unpooled): KVarN's V-row bit-width arms are NOT a pure bit ladder on this fixture.** Plain-quant flips-vs-f16 invert: b2 1.04% < b4 2.63% < b3 5.72%, and plain b3 PPL read −0.24% BELOW the f16 base while b4 read +0.13%. The arms are qualitatively different quantizers (b2 = skip-varn + grouped-4 RTN; b3/b4 = var-norm + per-ROW absmax RTN), and for outlier-heavy V rows the per-row step at b3/b4 can exceed the per-group step at b2 — the absmax caveat in another costume. Gate 1's per-layer MSE table is the evidence. This belongs to KVarN's V-tile posture (katgpt-rs side), not to the P1 question; recorded for the mining intake.

**Reading (honest, both directions):**

1. **Why the law overpredicts:** `1 − ρ` assumes the quantizer's noise scales with residual VARIANCE at a stationary range. KVarN's RTN scales by per-row (or per-group) absmax RANGE; mean removal changes the range too, and on outlier-carrying V rows the post-removal range does not shrink proportionally to the variance (recorded: encode range > raw range on 11/26 layers). The prediction is variance-exact and range-blind — the exact failure mode Bench 895's synthetic (iid, stationary-range) could not show.
2. **The b2 exception is the grouped posture, not the bits:** at b2 KVarN runs skip-varn + grouped-4 RTN (tight per-group ranges), and there mean removal is a wash-to-tiny-win. The mr-vs-plain comparison stays internally valid at every bits (matched bits, same quantizer); the CROSS-bit ladder is not a pure bit ladder (finding 2 above).
3. **PPL is a blunt instrument for a 0.1–0.5% surface:** plain b2 costs +0.083% PPL and mr-b2 −0.022% of that — both far under slice noise (the p-b3 arm read BELOW base by −0.24%, which is noise claiming a real effect). The flip/ΔNLL paired axes are the sensitive readouts; the gate verdicts used them (win share, mean paired ΔNLL) as pre-registered.
4. **Fixture caveats carry** (Bench 004): 288-tensor conversion, no q/k-norm; 61k-token calibration is a reduced-scale read (mean ρ_l(V) 0.526 vs 0.480 at Bench 004's 120k — the singleton-inflation direction).
