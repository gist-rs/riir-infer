## Verdict (per the pre-registered interpretation)

1. **The bar is MET at 4× compression at 64K — by the recency family.** max-recent 8/8 with meanΔ +0.243: the shallow six needles are at full-cache parity (|Δ| ≤ 0.06); only mid-deep needles 5/6 carry damage (+0.93/+0.84), both under EPS. diff meets the bar at the boundary (7/8) — needle 6 crosses EPS at +1.142.
2. **usage-rate collapses at 64K/25% (4/8)** with a perfectly monotone depth pattern (+2.89 at depth 0 → +0.009 at depth 50739): cumulative-mass/age evicts the oldest rows first, and at 64K the age spread is 4× the 16K cell's. Its needle survival 0.119 ≈ the tie base rate — the policy keeps almost no needle rows AND its kept filler doesn't backfill them (unlike 16K/50%, where spread retention carried it to 7/8).
3. **random reads 6/8 (+0.644) with needle survival 0.125 ≈ the 25% base rate** — uniform eviction keeps a proportional slice of everything; the DeltaNet layers carry enough of the rest that the null lands mid-pack. The null is the reason the count bar alone can't be the promotion readout: 6/8 beats usage's 4/8 here.
4. **diff vs maxrecent at 64K: NOT selection-identical** — needle 6 flips across EPS (+1.142 vs +0.841) while every other needle agrees to ~0.04. This is Bench 009's measured "λ perturbs near-tie filler selections with model-visible consequences", now observed at 64K with a NEGATIVE sign (at 8K/3pct the same perturbation was sign-inconsistent across budgets). The T7 mechanism holds to first order (d ≈ a — survivals 0.872 vs 0.878, meanΔ +0.286 vs +0.243); the perturbation is a tie-breaker, not a policy.
